## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes

## Instructions

1. **Prepare ingredients**: Gather all ingredients and measure them out.

   ![Step 1](step-1.jpg)

2. **Mix dry ingredients**: In a large bowl, combine flour and salt.

3. **Cook base**: Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Add garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute.

4. **Add tomatoes**: Add diced tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.

5. **Combine**: Add the tomato mixture to the flour mixture and stir until combined.

6. **Serve**: Serve hot with your favorite accompaniments.
