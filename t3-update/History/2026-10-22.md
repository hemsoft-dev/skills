## 09:03 - V1.3: fix the worker dying on an empty process list

The update ran with exit 3: T3 closed at 08:51 and Franz relaunched it via Ctrl+Shift+1, but the worker wrote no result.
Under `Set-StrictMode -Version Latest`, `(Get-T3Process).Count` throws when the function returns zero or one process
(the `@()` unrolls on return), so the close wait threw once T3 exited, and the catch's own `.Count` check killed the
worker. Call sites now wrap with `@(...)`. Parse-checked, `@(Get-InstallerProcess).Count` gives 0 under strict mode, and
`-Status` reports T3 running current, patched code. Retrospective: the cause only showed in the PowerShellCore/Operational
log (event 4100); worth checking first when a headless worker leaves no trace.
