**[Scaling long-running autonomous coding - Cursor's browser experiment](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jan/19/scaling-long-running-autonomous-coding/)**

Cursor ran hundreds of concurrent AI coding agents for nearly a week to build a web browser from scratch, writing over 1 million lines of code across 1,000 files.
