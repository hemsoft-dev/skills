## 🎯 Today's Highlight

**[Chrome takes on AI browsers with tighter Gemini integration, agentic features for autonomous tasks](https://techcrunch.com/2026/01/28/chrome-takes-on-ai-browsers-with-tighter-gemini-integration-agentic-features-for-autonomous-tasks/)**

Google is rolling out enhanced Gemini integration in Chrome, including a new "Auto Browse" autonomous agent feature that can handle tedious browsing tasks automatically. The agent is now available to AI Pro and AI Ultra subscribers, and Gemini in Chrome now connects to Gmail, Calendar, YouTube, Maps, Google Shopping, and Google Flights, with the ability to edit images directly from web pages. This represents Google's strategic move to compete with dedicated AI browsers like Arc and Opera's Aria by bringing agentic capabilities directly into the world's most popular browser.
