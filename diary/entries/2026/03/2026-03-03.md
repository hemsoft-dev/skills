I'm well underway on the SFL system. I did simplify today. It's now 10 p.m., and I'm at a really good spot, and I'm working through a simple workflow where I'm taking a to-do item and putting it onto the SFL system by having an issue created. The SFL skill is able to take a to-do item, create an issue out of it, and slap the correct labels on there, which kicks off the dispatcher. It kind of just funnels through the system that way, so that's what I'm currently monitoring. If that goes well, that is then the initial version of the SFL system. Tomorrow I will introduce that he is taking over the Foundation Chapter and that I will be focusing on the engineering track. That will be a nice relief, so I don't really have to plan much for tomorrow in that regard.

Otherwise, I'm wrapping up the daily diary here and checking on my other Todoist items for today. Overall, a really good day, and I even got to play a little 7 Days to Die.

On a separate note, after spending around six to seven hours with GitHub Copilot today, I am really taking a liking to GPT-5.3-Codex. It feels solid and precise without being overly eager, and I really appreciate the 400,000-token context window. It is quickly becoming my favorite model, and the fact that it is about three times cheaper than Opus makes that even better.

Over the last couple of weeks, I have had moments where Opus (and earlier Sonnet models) went too proactive and started running away with things. That is not always bad, but sometimes it is too much. GPT-5.3-Codex has been excellent for set-it-free loop work and for going deep on tricky Relias Assistant threading problems. This is likely my new go-to model going forward, and I am genuinely happy about it since it should cut my token spend to about a third.
