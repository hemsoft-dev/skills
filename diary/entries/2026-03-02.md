**Charter & Scope Review**

- Reviewed draft AI Task Force charter (authored by George) defining in-scope vs. out-of-scope areas
- In scope: shared understanding of AI usage, translating acceptable use policies into practical guidance, structured intake/prioritization for AI use cases, reducing duplication, documenting reusable patterns, making guidance accessible org-wide
- Out of scope (draft, reopened for discussion): building production AI systems, long-term operational support, approving AI tools/vendors/budgets, bypassing legal/security/IT/governance, acting as general AI help desk

**SDLC-Centered Strategy Debate**

- Debate on whether to limit initial efforts to SDLC stages under engineering control vs. holistic cross-departmental approach
- Pro focus: direct control, quick measurable outcomes, avoid credibility loss from unvalidated use cases
- Counter: support product management/UX/non-coding workflows, leverage AI for requirements quality, accessibility reviews, time tracking, self-evaluations, support triage

**Adoption & Authority (Franz's Contributions)**

- Raised concerns about advisory-only vs. actual workflow influence, risk of repeating low-adoption patterns, need for organizational buy-in and measurable outcomes
- Response: primary success metric = deliver more product with same/better quality; make AI-enabled paths the path of least resistance; champion successful teams over mandating compliance

**AI Use Cases Discussed**

- Agentic development tools and reusable AI "skills"
- AI-assisted CI/CD validation for skills
- AI to detect error deltas after staging/production deployments
- AI support for bug triage and observability
- Personal productivity use cases (time tracking, self-evaluations, reporting)

**Decisions**

- Charter and scope definitions are drafts, not final
- Avoid finalizing scope unilaterally — refine collaboratively
- Initial focus: validated, provable AI use cases where engineering has control

**Action Items**

- All members: contribute proven AI initiatives and use cases asynchronously to shared document with evidence of value
- Task Force: review contributed use cases in future meeting, distinguish proven vs. unproven, decide what to scale
- Scope review: revisit in/out of scope as a group
