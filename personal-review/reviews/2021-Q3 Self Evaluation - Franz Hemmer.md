## Self-evaluation, Q3, 2021

This past quarter I worked on the Microsoft Legacy Platform Optimization workstream with two goals in mind.

- Automate Schema Compares done by release owners.
- Improve the developer setup experience.

These goals have been met with the implementation of Azure DevOps Pipelines that automate both Schema Compares and EF Migrations for all our services. Also, with the establishment of a DevTest Labs environment, we can now provision Virtual Machines that have development software, Relias repositories and Relias databases prepopulated giving the developer a faster route to being productive. On top of that, we have the architecture in place for a configuration tool to automate the setup process. One of the first things the configuration tool is capable of is provision a service bus for the developer that automates the creation of queues, topics and subscriptions.

I have enjoyed working with Microsoft and have learned and grown a lot of the past quarter. I have become proficient with Azure DevOps Pipelines, DevTest Labs and PowerShell as a result of the work that has been accomplished this last quarter.

I look forward to that what is next and the great things we can accomplish helping developers and release owners to become more productive.

My goal for the next quarter is to continue to improve the DevTest Labs developer experience, automate as much as is possible to make onboarding and maintenance of developer machines easier. Also, presenting in chapter meetings on relevant topics is an ongoing goal for me.

Some initiatives that I’d like to pursue going forward is the lack of Technical Documentation around our RLMS platform which presents some problems around finding a source of truth when it comes to complex business rules.

Further, I believe we need to do a better job of onboarding developers and giving them the opportunity to learn our architecture and technology stack. Based on a presentation on how to maintain our development environment to a group of developers, it became clear to me that there is a big knowledge gap that we should help fill.
