## Self-evaluation, 2023

**Key Accomplishments:**

Throughout my time on the Open Sourcerers team until the end of this year, our team has been diligently tackling the challenges of integrating the Legacy system with the Target Architecture. I took the lead in shaping a clear roadmap for the User Profile Service, laying out a path forward for Version 2.

Version 2 has Dapr (Distributed Application Runtime) at its foundation and I have completed a Best Practice solution for Dapr in the relias-best-practice repository.

**Challenges &amp; Opportunities:**

Formalizing the entities that form a User in the new Target State along with the fact that we would like Users to be able to be part of multiple Organization creates some challenges to the design and the workflows. Hashing these entities out will be vital to the success of future User Management.

One important opportunity identified is using Dapr as a wrapper for our API’s. I continue to push for this vital technology as it will dramatically improve the quality of our API’s.

**Collaboration &amp; Leadership:**

While on the Open Sourcerers team we actively collaborated with HP, Architecture and Integration teams on the authentication and integration aspects.

As part of leading Open Sourcerers I am also continuing to participate and contribute actively to our weekly Architecture meetings to form best practices and talk through approaches for our journey towards Target State.

**Summary:**

Towards the end of the year, I joined the Developer Experience team underneath Productivity Engineering. We have already made monetary gains finding viable Feature Flag alternatives that will save us at least $50K/year moving forward. Negotiations are still underway.

I look forward to my work with Develop Experience and would love leading the team in a more formal capacity when the manager position opens for our team.

I believe there is huge potential for us in maturing our Engineering organization!
