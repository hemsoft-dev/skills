## Self-evaluation, 2017

During 2017, my team and I have fully integrated into the Relias Agile process and are now a functioning part of the Relias Engineering team. It has been fun to learn the process and I feel like it is an effective way for us to communicate, write and test software.

Throughout the year I’ve worked on a variety of areas such as:

- Azure Media Services POC.
- Ardent event occurrence import.
- Visual Studio 2017 research.
- Developed two new reports – PATHS Status and Assessment Detail.
- Enabled Pro-On-The-Go to be assignable in the RLMS.
- Made it possible to use seed data for integration tests in the Reporting Service.
- AIS Migration Gaps – Most notably the caching of report parameters between Legacy and Angular, enabling the reports to be editable on the Legacy side, and viewable on the Angular side.
- Developed the data model and implementation of Question Domains for AIS.
- I learned, owned and co-owned the Release once during 2017, and will continue this work this year.

In 2017 I have learned more about Angular and Azure which were smart goals for me during this year. This is a continuous process, and I look forward to learn more about these fields during the coming year.

I feel like I’ve had a successful 2017 and contributed in a variety of important areas for Relias. I’m looking forward to the next year.
