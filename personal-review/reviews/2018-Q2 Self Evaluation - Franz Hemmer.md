## Self-evaluation, Q2, 2018

This past quarter I worked on several different areas and projects:

- Worked on delivering all committed Q2 MDR’s plus one for Q3 along with customer reported bugs.
- Continue to improve Service Dashboard as new services are created or modified.
- Owned the 5/31 release.
- Wrapped up AIS Migration work.
- Worked on getting .NET Core 2.1 into all environments including a production release this and next week.
- Worked with Systems Management on a few issues like Redis Cache setup for Internal Tools and troubleshooting issues with environments.

My goal for Q3 is to help the team during sprints and to try and improve the Release process which is becoming increasingly complex as new services are introduced. I will push the idea that we are in strong need of a dedicated Release Owner role in our company as cycling this responsibility among the teams is high risk. A dedicated Release Owner can not only get close familiarity with the process, but also have the necessary expertise to improve the process which is very needed.
