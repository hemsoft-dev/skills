## Self-evaluation 2020

Wow! What a year 2020 was. Can’t say that I am unhappy to close that chapter.

2020 was a difficult year on many fronts and 2021 is probably going to be another difficult year, but hopefully there is light at the end of the tunnel.

2020 started off with continued visits to Morrisville to meet with Sr. Engineers and talk about our microservices architecture. We are still working through those challenges and will for the foreseeable future.

During the year I worked on a multitude of projects – Swank Gaps, PL Reports, Assessments, Barcode Scanning, Legacy Enterprise functionality, SDL, Enrollment Context API and the Required Training Page.

I owned two successful releases in 2020 and more to come in 2021. I will continue to repeat my plea for a dedicated release team.

On the training front I went through the Azure Fundamentals training and passed the Azure Fundamentals exam. I completed my first pass on “AZ-204 Developing for Microsoft Azure” and also looked at “AZ-304 Azure Architecture” before shifting focus towards Security related courses as part of working on our new Enrollment Context API.

As I mentioned last year, Service Dashboard continues to play a vital part of helping developers and release owners be more productive and continues to evolve with a growing number of features.

I have expressed an interest in Architecture or potentially a managerial role down the road and would be interested in working towards that in 2021. I have ambitious goals setup to take, complete and pass the “AZ-304 Azure Architecture” exam this year.

In summary, 2020 was another year full of changes. With OKR’s and empowered teams we have much to learn in 2021 but I feel like it is the right way to go. I feel energized by the challenges ahead and we are marching in the right direction!
