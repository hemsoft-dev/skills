## Self-evaluation 2021

This past year a lot has been accomplished. At the beginning of the year, I was the main contributor completing the Enrollment Context API and worked on various platform bug fixes alongside improvement to the Service Dashboard. I enjoyed working with Scrummy Bears and owned 2 releases before transitioning to form the Open Sourcerers team.

The first focus for Open Sourcerers was to work on the Legacy Optimization workstream. As part of the workstream we accomplished two main goals:

- Automated Schema Compares to make lives easier for release owners.
- Improved the developer setup experience by creating a DevTest Lab environment.

I have enjoyed working with Microsoft and have learned and grown a lot this past year. I have become proficient with Azure DevOps Pipelines, DevTest Labs and PowerShell as a result of the work that has been accomplished this last quarter.

Towards the end of the year I started working with Divum as a Mentor.

I look forward to continuing our partnership with Microsoft and working on improving our API infrastructure.

My goal for this year is to gain expertise in Azure API Management, and to understand all the best practices around that and APIs in general. I have several courses lined up on Pluralsight and Udemy to go through.

I would like to reiterate two main areas from last year:

- Lack of Technical Documentation around our RLMS platform which presents some problems around finding a source of truth when it comes to complex business rules.
- Onboarding of developers is cumbersome still and the work with Divum has illustrated that. We need to allocate resources to maintaining our DevTest Lab environment and improve it for it to be truly successful.

I look very much forward to what we can accomplish this year on our journey towards the target architecture!
