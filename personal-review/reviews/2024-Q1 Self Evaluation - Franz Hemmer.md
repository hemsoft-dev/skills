## Self-evaluation, Q1, 2024

**Key Accomplishments:**

During the first quarter the focus has been on recommending an Internal Developer Portal for our Engineering department. This tool will provide much-needed insight into our inventory of software and the quality of our code. It will also give us the ability to collect metrics and track those metrics over time. We arrived at Cortex based on analysis of many leading Internal Developer Portals and it came out on top in almost all areas.

Also, I have been pushing to improve the onboarding process by picking up the Configurator tool I wrote a couple of years back and modernizing it to automate some key processes as they relate to setting up the LMS system on a developer machine. Sagar and I made significant progress in this area, and we are continuing this work in the next quarter.

**Challenges &amp; Opportunities:**

With Cortex being approved we have a unique opportunity in front of our team to make Cortex the vehicle by which we will improve the quality of our code base and help motivate teams to continuously make improvements that will help make our organization more mature.

**Collaboration &amp; Leadership:**

As part of our team’s mission to help improve the developer experience and to drive productivity improvements it is only natural that we collaborate with teams and communicate openly about our roadmap and priorities. Thus, collaborating with the teams is crucial for our success. It should be front and center in everything that we do.

**Summary:**

We’re off to a great start and I enjoy working with my team mates and feel we have a great dynamic and synergize wonderfully. I’m excited to start implementing Cortex in the current quarter and expect we will reap great benefits from using it.
