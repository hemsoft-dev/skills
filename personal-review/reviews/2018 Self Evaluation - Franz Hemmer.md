## Self-evaluation 2018

During 2018 I have worked on a number of projects and areas:

- Owned the 1/25, 5/31 and 8/23 releases.
- Owned hotfixes 1/30 and 4/12 and assisted in others.
- Completed the MDS-RAI project.
- Completed AIS Migration work.
- Got 2 nd place in the 2018 Hackathon-I for Release Tool and Service Dashboard.
- Created and demoed Service Dashboard as an opportunity to automate developer workflow around our services and am continuing to improve and maintain the product.  It has become a standard tool for many developers to fire up our services. One great addition was the automatic generation of diffs, automating one step in the release process.
- Worked throughout the year and delivered MDR’s on time along with customer reported bugs.
- Worked on getting .NET Core 2.1 into all environments including a production release.
- Worked with Systems Management on a few issues like Redis Cache setup for Internal Tools and troubleshooting issues with environments.
- Helped app support by adding a feature to the Internal Tools to automate Mailgun suppression list removals, which is a big time saver for app support.
- Worked on the Sharing Reports project. Most notably created a rollback script for unrolling pilot customers (should they choose to do so).
- Worked on the Hide Grades project.

My goal for 2019 is to continue to help the team during sprints and to try and improve the Release process which is becoming increasingly complex as new services are introduced. Personalized Learning will be a big focus of the beginning of 2019 for our team.

I will continue to push the idea that we are in strong need of a dedicated Release Owner role in our company as cycling this responsibility among the teams is high risk. A dedicated Release Owner can not only get close familiarity with the process, but also have the necessary expertise to improve the process which is very needed.
