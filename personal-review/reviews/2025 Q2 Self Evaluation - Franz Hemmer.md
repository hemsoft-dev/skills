## Self-evaluation, 2025 Q2

**Self-Evaluation, 2025 Q2**

**Overview of Accomplishments**

- **"Learn AI Together" Program Launch: Established a new company-wide AI learning initiative originally piloted as "Lunch &amp; Learn AI". Due to overwhelming demand and strong participation (often exceeding 50 engineers), the program was promoted to a prime-time slot and renamed "Learn AI Together." Feedback has been overwhelmingly positive, and the initiative is now a cornerstone of AI enablement across Engineering.**
- **GitHub Migration Progress: Continued to lead the GitHub migration effort, which is progressing strongly. Nearly 100 repositories have now been onboarded to GitHub, significantly improving collaboration and standardization across engineering teams.**
- **Legacy Release Tools: Delivered the first beta version of the Legacy Release Tools suite. This marks a major milestone, with ongoing iteration planned in Q3 to refine features, address bugs, and prepare for full rollout.**
- **Relias Assistant (Yakob!): Advanced the Relias Assistant project closer to real-world application. Azure resource deployments are underway, and the team is on track for a limited internal test phase in Q3. The assistant will serve as an AI-driven helper tailored to Relias workflows.**
- **Cortex Platform Enhancements: Continued to provide deep feedback and partnership with the Cortex team. Notable progress includes dashboard expansion with DORA metrics and a vastly improved Home Dashboard that consolidates high-impact data like PR overviews, streamlining developer awareness and productivity.**
- **Configurator Improvements: Ongoing development and support of Configurator has reinforced its position as a vital tool for developer onboarding and workstation management. It remains a key part of our internal productivity tooling.**

**Future Focus**

- **GitHub Migration Completion: See the migration through to 100% completion, ensuring all teams are fully transitioned and operating smoothly.**
- **AI Enablement at Scale: Continue evolving "Learn AI Together," keeping content fresh and engaging, and exploring ways to further integrate AI into day-to-day workflows.**
- **Relias Assistant Testing: Launch the internal pilot of the Relias Assistant and gather actionable feedback to guide iteration.**
- **Legacy Tools Iteration: Enter structured testing and feedback cycles for the Legacy Release Tools, with a goal of reaching general availability by the end of Q3 or early Q4.**
- **Tooling Optimization: Continue iterating on Cortex and Configurator to ensure both platforms scale with the business and adapt to evolving engineering needs.**

**Summary**

**This quarter has been defined by strong execution and continued momentum across strategic initiatives. From scaling AI education to pushing core productivity tools forward, I’ve focused on delivering high-impact outcomes through a mix of leadership, innovation, and cross-functional collaboration. These efforts are contributing meaningfully to the future-readiness of Relias Engineering.**
