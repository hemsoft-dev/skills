## Self-evaluation, Q2, 2023

**Key Accomplishments:**

During the second quarter our team has continued navigating the complexities of coupling the Legacy system to Target Architecture and I lead the effort to formalize a Version 2 path forward for the User Profile Service.

Our team has also completed a handover to the Integration Team for the first version of our User Profile Service.

**Challenges &amp; Opportunities:**

Formalizing the entities that form a User in the new Target State along with the fact that we would like Users to be able to be part of multiple Organization creates some challenges to the design and the workflows. Hashing these entities out will be vital to the success of future User Management.

One important opportunity identified is using Dapr as a wrapper for our API’s. I continue to push for this vital technology as it will dramatically improve the quality of our API’s.

**Collaboration &amp; Leadership:**

We are collaborating with the TDD and Integration teams on the authentication and integration aspects and we have continued good lines of communication with one another.

As part of leading the team I am also continuing to participate and contribute actively to our weekly Architecture meetings to form best practices and talk through approaches for our journey towards Target State.

**Summary:**

This past quarter has been another productive one. We have carved a way forward for a Version 2 of the User design and will be working actively on that the next quarter.
