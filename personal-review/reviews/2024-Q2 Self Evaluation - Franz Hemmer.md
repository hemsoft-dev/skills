## Self-evaluation, Q2, 2024

**What Went Well Last Quarter** •    Successfully launched Cortex, our Internal Developer Portal, and onboarded all teams.
•    Established comprehensive documentation for Cortex initiatives, including managing, planning and executing the onboarding of all of the Platform Engineering teams. Our team also onboarded a lot of services into the Cortex catalog as a result of this work.

•    Took initiative to get APIM downgraded, resulting in cost savings of approximately $220K/year.

•    Continuous improvements to the onboarding experience centered around many improvements to the Configurator application.

**What Didn’t Go So Well Last Quarter** •    Layoffs negatively impacted morale across the teams.
•    Collaboration with Accenture encountered challenges. It appears to me to be a symptomatic issue for Relias to work with consulting companies.
•    Communication around the target architecture strategy and architecture appears challenging.

**Plans for the Next Quarter** •    Focus on maturing Cortex to enhance its capabilities and benefits for the teams.
•    Streamline and improve the onboarding process to ensure a smoother transition for new users.
•    Address communication gaps, especially within the Developer Experience group, and clarify the target architecture strategy.
