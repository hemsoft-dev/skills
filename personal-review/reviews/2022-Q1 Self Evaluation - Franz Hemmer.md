## Self-evaluation, Q1, 2022

This past quarter I started on Phase II of the Platform Modernization project, focused on API Management that will enable us to be agile on multiple fronts:

- Structure our API’s and be able to transition into our Target Architecture.
- Implement Policies.
- Adhere to security standards.
- Provide a Developer Portal to both internal and external developers.

There has been a lot of learning and discovery happening over the last quarter and I have learned a lot about API Management and APIs in general.

Also, I have promoted several initiatives to the Developer Experience as it is increasing in complexity each year. The introduction of DevTest Labs last year has provided a few improvements in this area, but lots of improvements remain. I’ve had several meetings and presentations on this this past quarter.

I have helped onboard team members to Open Sourcerers and done several technical interviews.

I’m enjoying our partnership with Microsoft and look forward to learning and discovering more and I’m eager to get started on implementing APIM in our new environment when it is available.
