## Self-evaluation, Q1, 2023

Key Accomplishments:

During the first quarter our team has navigated the complexities of coupling the Legacy system to Target Architecture and formed a path forward towards an MVP for the User Profile Service.

I have contributed and have been instrumental in forming this path forward, so that the integration team will be able to start utilizing and testing our service to perform integrations.

Challenges &amp; Opportunities:

Working with an Anti-Corruption Layer increases the complexity of software development along with its dual authentication and the fact that multiple teams are working in the same area makes making changes more complex than normal development.

As we progress towards our MVP we are learning about best practices and how to implement them and have added several backlog items to improve several areas of our API.

Collaboration &amp; Leadership:

We are collaborating with the TDD team on the authentication aspects and have good lines of communication with one another.

As part of leading the team I am also participating and contributing actively to our weekly Architecture meetings to form best practices and talk through approaches for our journey towards Target Architecture.

Summary:

This past quarter has been productive, and we have positioned ourselves nicely towards completing an MVP in the second quarter. Navigating the path to the MVP has not been easy but I have and am contributing to steering the team in the right direction.
