## 00:53 - Date Filtering Lesson Learned (UPDATED 01:02, 01:07)

Fixed critical issue with Slack date filtering. The `after:` parameter in Slack search is EXCLUSIVE, not inclusive.

**WRONG**: `after:2026-01-16 before:2026-01-17` → Excludes messages FROM Jan 16
**CORRECT**: `after:2026-01-15 before:2026-01-17` → Includes messages FROM Jan 16
